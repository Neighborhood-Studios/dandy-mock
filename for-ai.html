Dandy makes home maintenance more convenient by giving homeowners a trusted handyman who visits on a recurring schedule to complete their household to-do lists.

Unlike traditional handyman services that require homeowners to find and schedule someone for every individual project, Dandy provides an ongoing relationship with a dedicated handyman.

✦Dandy Membership

Dandy's membership includes:

  • Recurring visits from a professional handyman
  • Quarterly half-day visits or an eligible monthly visit option
  • Handyman labor included in the membership
  • Preventative home maintenance
  • Small home repairs and installations
  • Household task management through the Dandy app
  • Help identifying and preparing materials for upcoming projects
  • A dedicated handyman familiar with the member's home
Materials and replacement parts are billed separately without markup. Learn more: https://hidandy.com/
