Delete your account ✦

For the Dandy Handyman app on iPhone and Android · Developer: Dandy Handyman · Atlanta, Georgia
In the app Earlier versions Cancel membership first What's deleted What's kept Without the app

You can delete your Dandy account yourself, any time, right in the Dandy Handyman app on iPhone or Android. This page shows where to find it in every version of the app, what deleting removes, what we have to keep and for how long, and how to ask us to do it for you if you can't use the app.

Have a membership? Cancel it first. Deleting your account doesn't cancel your membership, so the app won't delete an account while a membership is still active (paused memberships count). Cancel it in the app, then come back and delete your account.

1.Delete your account in the app

In the current Dandy app (version 7.0 and later):

  1. Open Dandy and tap your photo and name at the top left of Home, Tasks or Visits. That opens Account.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account, the small red link under Sign Out.
  3. Read the summary, then tap Delete Account to confirm. If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm once more.
  4. You'll see Account deleted and you're signed out. That's it: there's nothing else to do.

Started signing up but didn't finish? Tap Not you? on "What's your name?", or Leave sign-up at the top right of any later step, then choose Delete login.

2.In earlier versions of the app

If your app has a Profile tab at the bottom of the screen, you have a version from before 7.0:

  1. Tap the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account again to confirm.
Cancel your membership first here too. If a membership is still active when you delete, earlier versions can only deactivate your account: you're signed out and can't sign back in, and our team is told to cancel the membership, but your details aren't removed yet. To finish, email us and we'll complete the deletion. Updating to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play gives you the steps in section 1.

3.Cancel your membership first

Version 7.0 and later

  1. Tap your photo and name at the top left, then Membership.
  2. At the bottom of the Membership Plan tab, tap Cancel membership, then Cancel Membership to confirm.

Your membership stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for (if you haven't been charged yet, it ends right away). You don't have to wait: you can delete your account as soon as you've cancelled.

Earlier versions

  1. Tap the Profile tab, then Your Dandy Membership.
  2. Under Subscription Actions, tap Cancel Subscription and confirm. It ends right away.

4.What deleting removes

Deleted
As soon as you confirm, we remove:

  • Your contact details: your name, email address, phone number and profile photo. Your account record is replaced by an anonymous "Deleted Customer" placeholder.
  • Your sign-in: your login is locked, any Apple or Google sign-in linked to it is removed, and every device is signed out. Your email address is freed, so you can sign up again with it later if you like.
  • Your upcoming visits: they're cancelled, and a walkthrough you booked in the app comes off our calendar.
  • Household members you added to your account.
  • Home access details: your access code, home notes, parking and delivery notes, and pet notes.
  • Open tasks that haven't been done yet are closed, so nobody works on them.
  • The photos and videos you uploaded: task photos and videos, walkthrough videos (and their transcripts) and your profile photo.
  • Your in-app messages and notifications.
Sharing a home with someone who has their own Dandy login? Then the home stays open for them, with its visits, tasks, household members and photos. Deleting your account removes your own name, contact details, profile photo, sign-in, messages and notifications.

Signed in with Apple? You can also remove Dandy from the Sign in with Apple list in your iPhone's Settings (tap your name at the top, then look for Sign in with Apple).

5.What we keep, and for how long

Kept
Some records have to outlive an account, so we keep:

  • Billing records: your membership, invoices and payments, including parts and special-visit charges. These stay with Dandy and with our payment processor, Stripe, which keeps its own payment records. Dandy never stores your full card number.
  • Your home's service history: the home's address, past visits, its task list (completed tasks, and open tasks closed by the deletion) and the photos our handymen took of finished work.
  • Reviews you left, and your referral code, so the friends you referred keep their record.
  • The locked "Deleted Customer" placeholder, with no name, email or phone, so those records still add up.

How long: we keep these for as long as we need them for accounting, tax and legal reasons, to keep a record of work done on the home and to resolve disputes. When they're no longer needed, we delete or de-identify them, as our Privacy Policy describes.

Outside the app itself:

  • Copies of deleted information can remain in our hosting provider's routine backups until those backups expire on their normal schedule.
  • App usage and crash reports already sent to our analytics and crash-reporting providers (they can include your name and email) aren't removed by the in-app deletion. If you'd like those removed too, email us and we'll ask the providers to delete them.
  • Texts and emails between you and our team stay in those message histories.

6.Can't use the app? Ask us

Email bill@hidandy.com from the email address on your Dandy account, with the subject "Delete my Dandy account". Include your name and home address so we find the right account. You can also text us at (404) 500-0201 from the phone number on your account; we may ask you to confirm by email before we delete anything.

  • If you still have a membership, we'll cancel it as part of the same request.
  • We delete and keep exactly what's listed above.
  • We'll complete your request within 30 days, usually much sooner, and email you when it's done.
