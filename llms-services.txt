# Dandy handyman services

Dandy helps homeowners complete common home maintenance tasks, including:

## Preventative maintenance
- HVAC filter replacement
- Smoke detector battery replacement
- Weatherstripping
- Door and window maintenance
- Basic household maintenance checks

## Repairs
- Door adjustments and hardware repairs
- Cabinet adjustments
- Minor drywall repairs
- Caulking and sealing
- Basic plumbing fixture replacement
- Minor household carpentry

## Installations
- Curtain rods and blinds
- Mirrors and artwork
- Shelving and wall hooks
- Light fixtures and ceiling fans
- Faucets and other basic fixtures
- Home accessories

## Home projects
- Furniture adjustments
- Paint touch-ups
- Mounting and hanging
- Small home improvement projects
- General household punch lists

## Scope
Services are subject to Dandy's scope, safety requirements, and available visit time. Complex electrical, plumbing, roofing, and major renovation work are excluded. When a project is too large or specialized, the Dandyman can often handle part of the work, help diagnose the issue, or guide the member toward the right licensed specialist.
