Help & support

How can we help? ✦

Questions about your home, your visits or the Dandy app? Reach a real person on our Atlanta team, or find a quick answer below.

Text us (404) 500-0201 The quickest way to reach us. It's the same number the app's Contact Us button texts. Email bill@hidandy.com For anything that needs more detail, like a billing question or a data request.
In the app Contact Us Tap your photo and name at the top left, then Contact Us to start a text to our team.

We reply as soon as we can. Please include the name or email on your Dandy account so we can find it quickly.

Quick answers

Steps are for the current Dandy app (version 7.0 and later). If your app has a Profile tab at the bottom, you have an earlier version: look for the "Earlier versions" note.

1.Signing in+

Sign in with the email and password you joined with. If you joined with Continue with Apple or Continue with Google, use the same button again.

  • Forgot your password? Enter your email, tap Continue, then Forgot Password? We'll email you a link to set a new one. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive in a few minutes.
  • Seeing "Account deactivated"? Text or email us and we'll sort it out.

Earlier versions: tap Forgot Password? on the log-in screen.

2.Updating the app+

Get the latest version of Dandy from the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android). Open the listing and tap Update if you see it. Turning on automatic updates in your phone's store settings keeps you current.

If the steps on this page don't match what you see, updating usually fixes that.

3.Membership & billing+

Tap your photo and name at the top left, then:

  • Membership: see your plan, change plan, pause, resume or cancel, and manage your cards on the Payment Methods tab.
  • Invoices: your membership invoices and any parts and supplies.

When you cancel, your membership stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for. Questions about a charge? Email or text us.

Earlier versions: Profile tab → Your Dandy Membership for your plan, and Billing for invoices and payment methods.

4.Booking visits+

Open the Visits tab and tap Book a visit, pick a day and time, and confirm. Already booked? The same button reads Reschedule upcoming visit so you can move it. Booking needs an active membership.

Need something small that can't wait for your next visit? Text us and we'll see what we can do.

Earlier versions: tap Book Visit on the Home tab.

5.Tasks+

Your task list is how your Dandyman knows what your home needs. On the Tasks tab:

  • Add a task to describe something that needs doing. A photo or short video helps us bring the right parts.
  • Explore tasks to browse common jobs and add them to your list.

Earlier versions: use the To-do's tab.

6.Your account & data+

Update your details under your photo and name → User Profile and Home Profile.

Want to leave? You can delete your account in the app, or ask us by email. See Delete your account for the steps, what's removed and what we keep.
