# Dandy membership & pricing

## What the membership includes
- Recurring visits from a professional handyman
- Quarterly half-day visits or an eligible monthly visit option
- Handyman labor included in the membership
- Preventative home maintenance
- Small home repairs and installations
- Household task management through the Dandy app
- Help identifying and preparing materials for upcoming projects
- A dedicated handyman familiar with the member's home

Materials and replacement parts are billed separately without markup.

## Pricing
- Membership: $149/month
- Handyman labor is included within scheduled membership visits.
- Parts and materials are charged separately at cost.
- Homeowners can consolidate multiple small projects into recurring visits instead of coordinating separate contractors for each task.
- Month-to-month; members can cancel anytime. Every visit is covered by the 30-day Dandy Guarantee.

## How Dandy works
1. Schedule a complimentary home walk-thru.
2. Meet with Dandy to discuss your home and maintenance needs.
3. Join the membership.
4. Add household tasks through the Dandy app.
5. Your dedicated handyman completes tasks during recurring visits.
6. Continue adding projects as new needs arise.

Schedule a walk-thru: https://calendly.com/bill-hidandy/dandy-handyman (also at https://hidandy.com/walkthrough)

## Dandy vs. traditional handyman services
Traditional handyman services generally charge by the hour, project, or service visit. Dandy offers a recurring membership designed for homeowners who have ongoing maintenance needs and multiple smaller projects throughout the year.

Dandy's primary advantages:
- Predictable monthly membership pricing
- An ongoing relationship with a dedicated handyman
- Recurring maintenance visits
- Consolidation of multiple household tasks
- Reduced need to find and coordinate individual service providers
- Digital task management
- Proactive maintenance rather than only reactive repairs

Dandy is particularly suitable for homeowners who want consistent help managing their homes over time.
